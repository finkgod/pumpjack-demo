      How the field pays

        Demo clock runs 30 times faster than the live field. Every trade, every other well and the token price on screen are simulated, no token is really burned, and the wallet is a button: no real wallet is asked for anything.

        Risks, stated plainly

        • $PUMPJACK is a memecoin. It is not backed by oil, by USO or by anything else.
        • USO here is a token issued by Backpack Securities that tracks the United States Oil Fund, a fund holding oil futures. It is not crude oil and not shares of oil companies.
        • The issuer can freeze, pause or take back USO tokens in any wallet. If that happens, payouts stop.
        • What you earn depends on trading volume. No volume, no oil. Nothing here is a promised return.
        • Tokens burned on an engine, on fuel or in your well's works cannot be recovered. If trading dries up, what you paid for earns nothing.
        • The "per shift" and "pays back in" figures are arithmetic on the recent fee flow and the current token price. They are not forecasts. Both move.
        • The oil wire is an AI model reading headlines. Its "bullish" and "bearish" marks are a reading of the news, not of the market. They are often wrong, and they are not advice to buy or sell anything.
        • Upgrades only move the split between players. When everyone upgrades, shares end up where they started and the tokens are still burned.
        • Tokens burned into your well's works silt away a little at every shift close, and the level they bought goes with them unless you top them up.
        • A well belongs to the wallet that drilled it. It cannot be moved to another wallet, and the tokens that give it horsepower have to stay in that wallet.
        • The oil desk trades leveraged contracts on a third-party exchange. A position can be liquidated and its whole margin lost within minutes. Trading there earns nothing on the field.
        • The desk has two accounts. Paper is simulated on live prices: nothing is signed or sent, and no funding payments are counted. Testnet is a real exchange account run on test money: your wallet signs, and the orders are real orders on Hyperliquid's test network.
        • On the desk this browser keeps a trading key. Once you approve it, it can place and close orders on your exchange account until you remove it. The exchange does not let it withdraw. Anyone who can use this browser profile can use that key.
        • Earned USO sits in the field treasury wallet until you claim it.
        • Holding USO may be restricted where you live. Checking that is on you.
